# FANOVA demo scenario: The Bell Under North Quay

**Status:** `LOCAL DEMO FIXTURE`. This is fictional sample material prepared for a repeatable product demonstration. It is not a creator partnership, a real pilot, a rights-holder permission statement, or evidence of market demand.

The scenario is intentionally self-contained. It uses no known entertainment characters, settings, brands, or third-party source text.

## Demo purpose

Show how a creator publishes a source story with visible boundaries, a fan submits a continuation linked to that source version, and the creator reviews it. The app's role switch is simulated; it is not user authentication.

## Creator story form

**Creator shown by the app:** `Demo Creator`

**Story title:**

`The Bell Under North Quay`

**Story text:**

```text
At North Quay, the flood bell had been silent for thirty-two years. The station beneath the quay was sealed after the river crossed the lower streets, and every new city map left a blank square where its entrance had been.

At 02:13, Mira Vale heard the bell through the floor of her repair shop.

It rang once, paused, then twice: the old harbour signal for a jammed floodgate. Mira found a brass service key inside her mother's toolbox and used it on a narrow stair hidden behind the disused ticket board.

The station below was dry. That was the first impossible thing. The second was a fresh chalk mark on the control cabinet: seven short lines, one longer line, then a question mark.

Mira turned the key. A speaker crackled.

"Is the upper channel still open?" asked a voice.

She knew it from the city's archive recordings. It belonged to Niko Renn, the signal keeper who had vanished in the flood thirty-two years earlier.

Mira looked at the bell rope. Dust covered it except for a clean handprint.

She did not answer. She wrote the question in her notebook and climbed back toward the quay.

Above her, the river was low. Beneath her, the bell rang again, this time once.

Someone was waiting for a reply.
```

## Fan contribution form

**Fan shown by the app:** `Demo Fan`

**Contribution title:**

`The Answer in Brass`

**Contribution text:**

```text
Mira returned to the station with the old harbour signal chart. She did not touch the gate controls. Instead, she tapped the brass key against the pipe: two quick beats, one slow.

The speaker answered with a burst of static, then three knocks from somewhere beyond the wall.

On the chart, that pattern meant: line damaged, message received.

Mira set the key against the speaker grille. "I can hear you," she said. "Tell me what you need."

For a long moment there was only the sound of water moving through the foundations.

Then Niko's voice came back, weaker than before.

"Don't open the gate. Find the second bell."

Mira looked up. The station's ceiling held one iron hook, empty except for a fresh circle in the dust.

She wrote the words in her notebook. When she climbed out, the river had begun to rise.

At North Quay, a second bell rang from somewhere the city map still showed as blank.
```

The contribution is intentionally short and leaves the source story's main question open. It is sample copy, not a submission from a real fan.
